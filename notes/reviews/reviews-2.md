# Sampler Box Reviews (2 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Nadia H.** - 5 stars
   The Kashmiri Kahwa with saffron and almond was a real treat. I saved it for a Sunday afternoon.

2. **Walter E.** - 3 stars
   Good teas, poor box. The lid would not stay shut and a pouch of Assam had spilled when it arrived.

3. **Sunita B.** - 4 stars
   Masala Chai was bold and cosy. Would love a bigger portion, one cup was not enough.

4. **Frank M.** - 2 stars
   Waited three weeks for delivery. No email to say it was delayed. The Earl Grey was good, though.

5. **Grace Y.** - 5 stars
   Jasmine Pearls were lovely, they unfurl like little flowers. I kept watching the glass.

6. **Imran Q.** - 4 stars
   The Ilaichi Chai is excellent. My only wish is that the sampler came with more than a single serving of it.

7. **Betty S.** - 3 stars
   Peppermint was fine. The brewing instructions were printed in tiny grey type that I could not read.

8. **Oliver R.** - 5 stars
   Darjeeling First Flush is now my morning tea. Bright, a little muscatel, and no bitterness.

9. **Leila F.** - 1 star
   Two of the six teas tasted stale to me. The Chamomile Honey had almost no smell at all.

10. **Henry P.** - 4 stars
    Ceylon Breakfast is a proper cuppa. Strong, clean, and a bargain at the sampler price.

11. **Anita D.** - 5 stars
    I gave the box to my mother and she called me to say how much she liked the Rooibos Vanilla. Gift success.

12. **Stanley K.** - 2 stars
    Instructions said to use boiling water for the Sencha. It came out bitter and I almost wrote it off. Please fix the card.

13. **Mei T.** - 4 stars
    White Peony was gentle and sweet. I enjoyed the Oolong too. The pouches were a bit hard to reseal.

14. **Carol J.** - 5 stars
    The whole box smelled wonderful when I opened it. Ilaichi and ginger, just lovely.

15. **Dennis W.** - 3 stars
    Nice flavours, but I do not see why I got four teas I do not drink and only one tea bag of the Assam I like.

16. **Zainab A.** - 5 stars
    Hibiscus Berry was tart and cheerful over ice. Great for a warm afternoon.

17. **Philip N.** - 2 stars
    The box arrived in a thin envelope-style mailer and was bent. Tea was OK, the packing was not.

18. **Rosa L.** - 4 stars
    Lemon Ginger was zippy and bright. I would drink it every day.

19. **Arjun S.** - 5 stars
    I am a student on a budget and this was a perfect way to try different teas. The Masala Chai is going into my regular order.

20. **Edna V.** - 3 stars
    Pleasant box. The Earl Grey was lovely but I wish the sampler had two bags of it instead of one.
