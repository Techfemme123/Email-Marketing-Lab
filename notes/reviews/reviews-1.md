# Sampler Box Reviews (1 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Margaret T.** - 5 stars
   The Ilaichi Chai was a joy. I opened the pouch and the cardamom smell filled the kitchen. Already ordered more.

2. **Dev P.** - 2 stars
   Lovely teas, but the box took 19 days to arrive. By then I had nearly given up on it.

3. **Joan R.** - 4 stars
   Darjeeling First Flush was light and floral, just what I like. Wish the sample had been bigger, I got only two cups.

4. **Samir K.** - 3 stars
   Assam was strong and malty, good with milk. The brewing card said "steep 3-5 minutes" for everything, which is not much help.

5. **Linda W.** - 5 stars
   Jasmine Pearls opened up beautifully in my glass teapot. My daughter and I fought over the last ones.

6. **Tom B.** - 1 star
   The box was crushed on one corner and two pouches had split. Tea all over the other tins. Disappointed.

7. **Priya N.** - 5 stars
   Masala Chai tasted like my grandmother's kitchen. Warm, spicy, and just the right amount of ginger.

8. **Harold F.** - 3 stars
   Earl Grey was fine but a bit perfumed for me. The Ceylon Breakfast was the star of the box.

9. **Aisha M.** - 4 stars
   Great variety. The Sencha was grassy and fresh. Instructions did not say what water temperature to use, so my first cup was bitter.

10. **George L.** - 2 stars
    Only one tea bag of the Kashmiri Kahwa! It was my favourite and then it was gone. Please send more of the good ones.

11. **Ruth A.** - 5 stars
    Peppermint was bright and clean after dinner. Lovely little gift to myself.

12. **Kevin O.** - 4 stars
    Nice packaging, felt like a present. The Rooibos Vanilla was sweeter than I expected but my kids loved it.

13. **Fatima S.** - 5 stars
    The ilaichi in the Ilaichi Chai is the real thing, whole pods and not dust. I will be buying a full tin.

14. **Bill H.** - 2 stars
    Shipping was slow and tracking did not update for a week. The teas themselves were nice enough.

15. **Chen L.** - 4 stars
    White Peony was delicate and soft. I would have liked a little more of it, the pouch was tiny.

16. **Doreen C.** - 3 stars
    Chamomile Honey was pleasant but a bit weak. Had to use two bags to get any flavour.

17. **Raj V.** - 5 stars
    Best sampler I have tried. Every tea was different and nothing felt like filler. Oolong was my surprise favourite.

18. **Pauline D.** - 1 star
    The instructions were so confusing I could not tell which tea was which. The labels had rubbed off in the post.

19. **Marcus J.** - 4 stars
    Lemon Ginger woke me up on a grey morning. Good zing without being sharp.

20. **Elsie G.** - 5 stars
    I am 82 and I loved this little box. The Hibiscus Berry was a lovely ruby colour and tasted fruity. Thank you.
