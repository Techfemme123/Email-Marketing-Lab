# Sampler Box Reviews (4 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Helen B.** - 5 stars
   Darjeeling First Flush with a slice of lemon in the afternoon. Heavenly.

2. **Omar F.** - 3 stars
   The Masala Chai was very good, but the delivery date kept changing. It came 18 days after I ordered.

3. **Dorothy S.** - 4 stars
   A gentle, pretty box. The Chamomile Honey helped me wind down in the evening. I wish the pouches were bigger.

4. **Jamal W.** - 2 stars
   Box was dented and one pouch of Ceylon Breakfast was open. I ended up with tea dust everywhere.

5. **Lucy H.** - 5 stars
   Ilaichi Chai is now my go-to. I have bought two full tins since the sampler.

6. **Ken A.** - 4 stars
   Good range. Oolong was smooth and a bit buttery. The card should say how many times you can re-steep it.

7. **Naomi D.** - 1 star
   The brewing instructions were confusing and I ruined two teas before working it out. A waste of a nice Sencha.

8. **Bernard L.** - 5 stars
   Fresh, fragrant, and generous. The Earl Grey is one of the best I have had in years.

9. **Shanti R.** - 4 stars
   Jasmine Pearls and Kahwa were the highlights. I wanted more of the Kahwa, it vanished in one sitting.

10. **Irene K.** - 3 stars
    Nice teas but my box took 13 days. When I asked about it, nobody replied for three days.

11. **Mohammed Z.** - 5 stars
    I shared the box with my coworkers and we all had a favourite. Mine was the Assam.

12. **Patricia G.** - 2 stars
    Rooibos Vanilla smelled artificial to me. The rest of the box was pleasant.

13. **Daniel C.** - 4 stars
    Lemon Ginger was bright. Peppermint was clean. Box was neat and tidy.

14. **Anya P.** - 5 stars
    The little cards with each tea's story were charming. Masala Chai was the winner.

15. **Frederick J.** - 3 stars
    Good tea, though the sampler needs to say how many cups each pouch makes. I guessed and got it wrong.

16. **Lakshmi N.** - 5 stars
    Opened the box and smelled cardamom straight away. Lovely. Ilaichi Chai is the real thing.

17. **Gwen T.** - 4 stars
    White Peony was the surprise. Sweet and mild. Too small a portion though.

18. **Hassan B.** - 2 stars
    The pouches were not sealed well and the Earl Grey had gone a bit flat. Shipping took two and a half weeks.

19. **Maggie O.** - 5 stars
    My husband and I tried one tea each evening for a week. A lovely ritual. The Hibiscus Berry was the hit.

20. **Leonard V.** - 4 stars
    Good quality, fair price. I would give five stars if the instructions were clearer on water temperature.
