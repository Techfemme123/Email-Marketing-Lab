# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice Guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea subscription box for people on our waitlist. Our readers are aged 40 to 90 and come from all walks of life: busy professionals, students and retired people. They want tea with good flavour, especially ilaichi (green cardamom), and a smell that grabs them from the first sip.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Charming. Warm. Chatty.**

Kettlebird sounds like a handsome, witty host who makes every guest feel special. Think of a cheerful morning chat over chai. We are never loud or pushy, and never boring or slow.

## 2. Words we use

- **friends** for our customers
- **fresh** and **fragrant** for the tea
- "aroma that touches the soul"
- **ilaichi** (green cardamom)
- We only name where the tea comes from (for example, the tea farms of Ceylon) if the supplier can prove it
- "an aroma that greets you before the first sip"
- **the magical box** and **the surprise** every month
- **The Steep Club**

## 3. Words we avoid

- Slang
- Fake-pressure words: "hurry", "limited time", "buy now"
- "Dear customer" (too stiff)
- ALL CAPS
- "!!!" or piles of exclamation marks
- More than one emoji per email

## 4. Claims we must never make

- That tea cures, heals or treats anything
- That tea helps you lose weight
- An exact delivery date guarantee
- That prices will never change
- That we are the "best", "No. 1" or "finest"
- That our tea is "organic" or "100% natural", unless we have proof

## 5. How we sign off

- Closing line: **Until your next cup**
- Sender: **Ara, from the Kettlebird team**
- Every email ends with a **P.S.** that shares a small brewing tip.
  Example: "P.S. Crush the ilaichi pods before brewing."

Sign-off example:

> Until your next cup,
> Ara, from the Kettlebird team
>
> P.S. Crush the ilaichi pods before brewing.
