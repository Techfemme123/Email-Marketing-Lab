# Sampler Box Reviews (5 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Clara M.** - 5 stars
   Ceylon Breakfast and Ilaichi Chai were both wonderful. Rich, comforting, and fresh.

2. **Vijay S.** - 3 stars
   Nice teas. Only one sachet of Masala Chai, and it is the one I wanted most. Slow delivery too.

3. **Eileen R.** - 4 stars
   Gentle, floral Jasmine Pearls. I did not enjoy the Peppermint as much, a bit sharp.

4. **Carlos D.** - 2 stars
   The outer box arrived soaked in the rain. Two pouches were wet and I threw them away.

5. **Hannah P.** - 5 stars
   Kashmiri Kahwa tasted like a holiday. Saffron, almonds and warmth. More please.

6. **Arthur L.** - 4 stars
   I am 78 and found the instructions easy to follow, mostly. Sencha was lovely.

7. **Tanvi K.** - 5 stars
   Student life is stressful, and this box made evenings feel special. The Chamomile Honey was soothing.

8. **Gordon N.** - 1 star
   Arrived a month late. I ordered it as a birthday gift and it missed the day. The tea is good but the timing was poor.

9. **Bella F.** - 4 stars
   The Rooibos Vanilla was a lovely caffeine-free treat. The sampler pouches were a little small.

10. **Rashid H.** - 5 stars
    Assam was strong, dark, and wonderful with milk. Ilaichi Chai was excellent too.

11. **Judith C.** - 3 stars
    Good flavours but the card said one thing and the pouch said another about steeping time. Confusing.

12. **Nathan B.** - 4 stars
    The Oolong had a lovely orchid note. I would like a bit more of it. The packaging was sturdy.

13. **Sofia G.** - 5 stars
    Earl Grey and Darjeeling were perfect. My mother-in-law asked where I got them.

14. **Terence W.** - 2 stars
    I could barely taste the White Peony. Followed the card to the letter. The rest were fine.

15. **Meera J.** - 5 stars
    The smell of the ilaichi when I opened the pouch was glorious. I closed my eyes for a moment.

16. **Cynthia A.** - 4 stars
    Lemon Ginger was refreshing. Delivery took 12 days, which was longer than I expected.

17. **Isaac T.** - 3 stars
    Okay for a taster. The Hibiscus Berry was too tart and I missed having more of the Ceylon.

18. **Winifred S.** - 5 stars
    Everything arrived in tidy little pouches. The box felt like a gift. I am so pleased.

19. **Faisal O.** - 4 stars
    Masala Chai and Kahwa were great. A little confusing that the brewing guide did not list all the teas.

20. **Doris E.** - 2 stars
    One tea bag each. I wanted to try each tea twice before deciding. Nice flavours, stingy amounts.
