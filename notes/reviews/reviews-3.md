# Sampler Box Reviews (3 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Gloria M.** - 5 stars
   The Ilaichi Chai made my whole morning. Sweet, spicy, and so fragrant.

2. **Neil T.** - 2 stars
   Shipping took over two weeks and the courier left it with a neighbour I do not know. Tea was good.

3. **Yusuf B.** - 4 stars
   Assam was full and malty. The Kahwa was a nice change. I would like a clearer brewing chart.

4. **Margaret O.** - 5 stars
   Every tea had its own little pouch with a label. Neat and tidy. The Darjeeling was exquisite.

5. **Paul D.** - 3 stars
   Sencha was good but I only got enough for one pot. I would have paid more for a larger sample.

6. **Hiroko S.** - 4 stars
   The Oolong had a gentle roasted sweetness. I found the brewing times a little vague.

7. **Sandra K.** - 1 star
   The tea arrived damp. The packaging had been torn and the Peppermint pouch smelled of cardboard.

8. **Vikram J.** - 5 stars
   Masala Chai with a splash of milk is perfection. My wife has now claimed the rest.

9. **Alice P.** - 4 stars
   Lovely Jasmine Pearls. The Chamomile Honey was a little too sweet for me, but that is just taste.

10. **Roger H.** - 2 stars
    Instructions were all over the place. One tea said 2 minutes on the pouch and 4 on the card.

11. **Ingrid L.** - 5 stars
    Earl Grey with real bergamot smell. It was like opening a little bottle of sunshine.

12. **Tariq M.** - 4 stars
    Ceylon Breakfast was robust. Wished there were two cups of it, it went too fast.

13. **Beverly C.** - 3 stars
    Good selection. The Rooibos Vanilla was nice but too sweet. Delivery was slow, almost 16 days.

14. **Ahmed R.** - 5 stars
    The ilaichi aroma hit me before I even lifted the lid. Such a good start to the day.

15. **Joyce F.** - 4 stars
    Lemon Ginger was refreshing. The box itself was pretty, I kept it for my sewing bits.

16. **Colin G.** - 2 stars
    The White Peony tasted like hot water to me. I followed the steps. Not for me.

17. **Preeti A.** - 5 stars
    Hibiscus Berry, cold brewed overnight. Gorgeous colour and fruity taste. A summer hit.

18. **Albert N.** - 3 stars
    Tea was fine. Tiny print again on the instructions, I needed a magnifying glass.

19. **Jenny W.** - 5 stars
    I bought this for myself as a treat after a long week. Worth every penny. The Kahwa was a standout.

20. **Samuel E.** - 4 stars
    Good value. Would buy again if the box had a bit more of the Assam.
