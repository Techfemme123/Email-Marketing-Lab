# Sampler Box Reviews (6 of 6)

Practice data: made-up reviews of the Kettlebird Tea sampler box.

1. **Rebecca L.** - 5 stars
   Ilaichi Chai is addictive. I have been crushing the pods before brewing and it smells amazing.

2. **Sanjay M.** - 4 stars
   Darjeeling First Flush was a delight. Delivery was slow, 14 days, but worth the wait.

3. **Edwin H.** - 2 stars
   Pouches were not labelled clearly. I could not tell the Assam from the Ceylon Breakfast.

4. **Tamsin R.** - 5 stars
   Jasmine Pearls and White Peony were so elegant. A lovely box for a quiet weekend.

5. **Bashir A.** - 3 stars
   The Masala Chai was strong and spicy, which I liked. The Earl Grey was weak. Only one cup of each.

6. **Margot D.** - 4 stars
   Chamomile Honey was soft and calming. The brewing card could be bigger and clearer.

7. **Peter K.** - 1 star
   The box was crushed flat and half the pouches had burst. I sent a photo and had no reply.

8. **Divya P.** - 5 stars
   Kahwa, Chai and Oolong were all superb. I am planning to order the full-size tins.

9. **Lionel S.** - 4 stars
   Rooibos Vanilla was smooth and pleasant. Sencha was grassy and bright. Good value overall.

10. **Katherine W.** - 3 stars
    The teas were nice. The shipping label said 5 days but it took 15. I would try again if it were faster.

11. **Amir J.** - 5 stars
    Peppermint was crisp and clean. Lemon Ginger was a great pick-me-up. Everything tasted fresh.

12. **Phyllis B.** - 4 stars
    Lovely presentation. I wish there had been a bit more of the Jasmine Pearls to share.

13. **Gabriel N.** - 2 stars
    The Hibiscus Berry was very sour and the instructions did not mention sweetening it. Rest of the box was good.

14. **Nirmala T.** - 5 stars
    The Ilaichi Chai took me straight back to my childhood in Kerala. Thank you for such a heartfelt blend.

15. **Stuart C.** - 4 stars
    Ceylon Breakfast was my favourite. I would have liked two servings of it, though.

16. **Olga F.** - 3 stars
    Good teas, but the little pouches were hard to open with arthritic fingers. A pull tab would help.

17. **Jerome V.** - 5 stars
    My first time trying loose leaf and this made it easy. Every tea was a pleasure.

18. **Hazel G.** - 2 stars
    Two of the pouches had leaked into the box and everything smelled of mint. The Earl Grey was spoiled.

19. **Rohan D.** - 4 stars
    Kashmiri Kahwa was lovely. The instructions said 2 to 6 minutes, which is a very wide range.

20. **Audrey M.** - 5 stars
    Charming, fragrant, and a pleasure to open. I have already told three friends about the box.
