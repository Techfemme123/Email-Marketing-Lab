# Skills I chose (Step 1)

Searches run: `email marketing`, `email sequence`, `campaign plan`, `resend`.
Nothing has been installed yet. Star counts were checked on GitHub on 2026-11-15.

## Use these

1. **For writing campaigns: `anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins`**
   (the `campaign-plan` and `email-sequence` skills)
   - Published by Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, so it is an official source.
   - The GitHub repo has about 36,000 stars and was updated two days ago.
   - It covers planning a campaign and writing the email sequence, which is what a studio needs first.

2. **For sending email: `resend/resend-skills`** (the `resend` skill; `resend/email-best-practices` is a good add-on)
   - Published by Resend, the company that runs the sending service, so the advice matches the real product.
   - Stars are modest (212 for resend-skills, 146 for email-best-practices), but the publisher is the authority here.
   - It is actively maintained, with commits in the last few weeks.

Runner-up: `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (about 60,000 stars, lots of installs). It's popular and well regarded, but it's one person's repo, not a company's. It's a fine second opinion, just not my first pick.

## Avoid these

1. **`caffeinelabs/skills@extension-email-marketing`**
   - It shows 31,700 installs, but the GitHub repo has only 2 stars. Those two numbers don't fit together.
   - I couldn't find a reason to trust the publisher, and a high install count alone proves nothing.

2. **`claude-office-skills/skills@email-marketing`**
   - The name sounds official, but it is not Anthropic.
   - The repo hasn't been updated since January 2026, so its advice on email rules and tools may be out of date.
   - Better, newer options exist from the publishers above.

"Avoid" here means I wouldn't trust them without reading them first. It doesn't mean they are proven harmful.

## How I decided

Check these three things before installing any skill:

1. **Who publishes it?** Prefer the company that owns the product or subject, such as `anthropics` for Claude or `resend` for Resend. Be careful with lookalike names.
2. **Do the numbers agree and are they fresh?** Compare the install count with the GitHub stars, and check when the repo was last updated. Lots of installs with almost no stars, or no updates in months, is a warning sign.
3. **What does it actually tell the AI to do?** Open the skill's page or `SKILL.md` and read it first. Look for anything odd, like running commands, reaching out to unfamiliar websites, or asking for passwords and API keys.
