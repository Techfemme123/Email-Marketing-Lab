- **Tea-curious beginners:** an easy start with no wrong answers. They worry it's too fancy or they'll choose wrong.
- **Daily tea drinkers:** better flavour, new finds and a monthly treat. They already have a favourite, so why add to it?
- **Gift buyers:** a thoughtful, personal gift. They worry about on-time delivery and whether it suits the person.
- **Angle A, a calm daily ritual:** a quiet, slow-morning cup. It speaks mainly to daily drinkers.
- **Angle B, discovering new teas:** a new fragrant tea every month. It speaks to curiosity and to the surprise in the box.
- **My pick: Angle B.** The plan's core message is the magical box and the surprise every month, and B says that directly.
- B answers the daily drinker's "why add more?" with something new. It also gives beginners a gentle way to explore and gives gift buyers a gift that keeps arriving.
- A fits one group well and leaves the other two with less to hold on to.
