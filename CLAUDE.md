# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` - Brand voice, logo notes, colors and style guidance.
- `leads/` - Lists and notes about prospects and customers.
- `campaigns/` - Campaign plans and email drafts.
- `notes/` - Ideas, research and meeting notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people. Drafts only.
- Sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in @resend.dev) and to my own email address. A guard will enforce this in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
